Terms of Service

These terms apply to authorized use of the MEDISENS Rural Health Unit information system.

Authorized access

MEDISENS may be used only by personnel with an assigned account and approved role. Access must be limited to legitimate healthcare and administrative duties.

User responsibilities

  • Keep account credentials confidential.
  • Enter and review information carefully.
  • Use patient information only for authorized purposes.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access or security incidents promptly.

Prohibited use

Users must not share accounts, attempt to bypass permissions, access records without a valid work purpose, alter audit information, or misuse system data.

Clinical responsibility

MEDISENS supports recordkeeping and approved workflows. Authorized healthcare personnel remain responsible for professional judgment, verification, and patient-care decisions.

Availability and updates

Access may be limited during maintenance, security response, or operational interruption. These terms may be updated when system or legal requirements change.
