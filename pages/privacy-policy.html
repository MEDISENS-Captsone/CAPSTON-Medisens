Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MEDISENS handles information used by authorized Rural Health Unit personnel.

Information handled

MEDISENS processes account, patient, clinical, operational, and audit information required to support authorized healthcare workflows.

How information is used

Information is used to provide clinical services, maintain patient records, support approved reporting, protect system security, and meet applicable legal responsibilities.

Access and protection

Access is limited according to assigned roles and permissions. Users must protect account credentials and may access patient information only for authorized work.

Retention and rights

Records are retained according to applicable healthcare, operational, and legal requirements. Requests concerning personal information should be directed to the authorized Rural Health Unit administrator.

Data Privacy Act

MEDISENS is intended to support responsible handling of personal information under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).
